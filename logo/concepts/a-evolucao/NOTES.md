# A. Evolução

## Idea
Today's mark, drawn with a ruler. Same black disc and speed-stripe V, but the hand-made wobble and the irregular notches are replaced by one rule: every stripe has the same height, the same gap and the same skew. Nothing new is added, so the equity of the current logo carries over.

## Construction (viewBox 512, 8-unit grid)
- Disc: true circle, r = 256, centred.
- V box: x 112 to 400 (288 wide, 56% of D), y 128 to 400 (272 tall). Both arms sit on the vertical axis x = 256.
- Skew: every V edge runs at 3:8, which is 20.56 degrees off vertical (today's arm measures about 21).
- Stripes: 6 rows, height 32, gap 16, pitch 48. All y values are multiples of 8, so every x is an integer.
- Right arm: solid, horizontal thickness 84 (= width of the flat bottom). It runs from y 128 to 304.
- Rows 4 to 6 (the lower three) span the whole V and close the point. The 4th row merges with the right arm, as the 5th did in the original.
- Counter (the notch between arms) ends at y = 288, 59% down the V.

## Clear space and minimum size
- Clear space: 0.25 D (128 units) on all sides.
- Minimum: 24 px disc. At 16 px the stripes blur into a grey texture and the V still reads, but use a hand-hinted favicon PNG there (as planned in A8).

## Why it fits
Zero rebrand risk and it keeps the "speed" idea (Ofício: measured, fast). The even rhythm also reads as engineering: a mechanical engineer's version of the GIMP original. Single-ink works as is: the V is knocked out of the disc.

## Risks
- Least distinctive of the three: it is today's logo, only cleaner.
- Loses the hand-drawn wobble that A8 wanted to keep. If the wobble matters, this concept is the wrong one.
- Fine stripes (16 units) do not survive below about 24 px.
