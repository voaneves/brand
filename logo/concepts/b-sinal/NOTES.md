# B. Sinal

## Idea
"V." The wordmark is now VOANEVES. with a Sinal full stop, so the symbol is the first letter plus that stop: a heavy V and a red dot on the baseline. Essence made visible: one clear signal, the rest is silence. The stripes are gone, which is the bolder move.

## Construction (viewBox 512, 8-unit grid)
- Disc: true circle, r = 256.
- V: x 142 to 358, y 148 to 364 (216 by 216). Edges at 3:8 (20.56 degrees), the same angle as concept A and the old logo. Arm thickness 72, inner counter ends 96 below the top.
- Flat bottom of the V is 54 wide, and the dot diameter is 54 (r = 27). The two share one measure.
- Dot: centre (340, 337). Its bottom sits on the V baseline (y 364). It tucks under the right arm with about 22 units of clearance to the arm edge.
- Group (V plus dot) spans x 142 to 367, centred on the disc.

## Clear space and minimum size
- Clear space: 0.25 D (128 units).
- Minimum: 16 px for the V. The dot is about 1.7 px at 16 px, so it reads as a speck; use it as an accent only from 24 px up.

## Why it fits
It is the clearest link between mark and wordmark: the red full stop appears in both. Works single-ink (the dot becomes a hole), reverse, and on Sinal. It stays legible at 16 px better than A.

## Risks
- A plain V. is generic. Distinction depends on the red dot and the 3:8 skew, so single-ink loses part of the idea.
- Loses the speed-stripe equity of the current logo.
- The dot is close to the edge of the V in single-ink at small sizes; check at 32 px before choosing.
