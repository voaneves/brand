Confirm before publishing; keep attribution either way. Brand marks (GitHub, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, YouTube) remain trademarks of their owners.
- Iconic (CC BY-SA 3.0, P.J. Onori) and Zondicons (CC BY 4.0, Steve Schoger) glyphs exist in the original font (moon-fill, translate) but none is in this folder. If an Iconic glyph is added later, derivatives must be shared under CC BY-SA 3.0 (share-alike), with attribution.
- Material Icons: Apache-2.0, include the license notice when redistributing.
- "In use" means used on the site: calendar-check, envelope-open, github, linkedin, adjust, translate, whatsapp, youtube. The other 11 are legacy and not used by the site.
