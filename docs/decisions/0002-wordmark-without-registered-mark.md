# 0002. Wordmark without a registered mark

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-14

## Context

VOANEVES is not registered at INPI, so the registered-trademark symbol would be inaccurate. A trademark symbol (TM) is also not wanted.

## Decision

Drop the registered-mark symbol everywhere and use no TM. The wordmark becomes "VOANEVES." with the full stop in Sinal, tying it to "Sinal, não ruído.". The logo may be fully redesigned (decision 4); three vector concepts are explored in M0 and one is chosen.

## Consequences

- Wordmark, lockups, docs and the board are updated accordingly.
- Marks stay under all rights reserved (see LICENSES.md) regardless of registration.
